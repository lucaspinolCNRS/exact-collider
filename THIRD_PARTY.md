# Third-party data

`index.html` embeds, and `data/planck_binned_shape.bin` stores, the Planck PR4 binned bispectrum
shape — the 171 bins, the measured shape on them and its inverse covariance — released with

O. H. E. Philcox, *What Shape is the Inflationary Bispectrum?*, arXiv:2603.17004,
<https://github.com/oliverphilcox/Binned-Bispectrum-Shape>.

It is not ours. It is redistributed here, repacked into one binary file, under the MIT licence of
that release, reproduced below. Please cite that paper for anything derived from the comparison with
the data.
